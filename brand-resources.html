Official FCI logo files

Use these files when you need the FCI logo, the FCI Community Housing logo, or the FCI CHS logo. They are the official artwork for partnerships, press and outsourced design. Download the variant that matches the background, and leave the artwork unchanged.

Download all logo files (ZIP, 354 KB)

How the logo must be used

  • 1
    Clear space. Leave empty space around the logo at least as tall as the letter F in “FCI”, on every side. Do not place type, photographs or borders inside that space.
  • 2
    Minimum size. Do not reproduce the full logo smaller than 120 pixels wide on screen or 30 mm wide in print, so “Community Housing Services” stays readable.
  • 3
    Do not alter it. Do not stretch, squash, recolour, rotate or crop the logo, and do not add outlines, shadows, glows or other effects.
  • 4
    Keep the background plain. Do not place the logo on busy photographs, patterns or strong gradients.
  • 5
    Keep contrast. Use Logo 1 or Logo 1B on light backgrounds and Logo 1A on dark backgrounds. If the wordmark does not stand out, switch variant instead of editing the file.

Primary mark · light backgrounds

Logo 1 — full colour

Logo 1 is the main FCI Community Housing Services logo. It is full colour: an amber symbol of four people around an olive house, with “FCI Community Housing Services” in deep teal. The PNG, SVG and PDF are transparent, so use them on white or another plain light background. On a dark colour the deep teal wordmark disappears. For dark backgrounds, use Logo 1A.

Reversed wordmark · dark backgrounds

Logo 1A — for dark backgrounds

Logo 1A keeps the amber and olive symbol and sets the wordmark in white. Use it only on dark, plain backgrounds, especially Deep Teal (#003333). The PNG, SVG and PDF are transparent: on white, the wordmark vanishes. The JPG already includes a dark teal background and can be placed as a finished image.

Full colour · light backgrounds

Logo 1B — for light backgrounds

Logo 1B is the full-colour logo prepared for light backgrounds: the same amber symbol, olive house and deep teal wordmark as Logo 1. It is supplied only as a JPG, and that file already includes a white background. Use it on white and other plain light backgrounds. There is no SVG, PNG or PDF for Logo 1B.
